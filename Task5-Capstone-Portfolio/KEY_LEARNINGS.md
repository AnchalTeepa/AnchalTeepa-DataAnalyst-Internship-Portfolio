# Key Learnings & Technical Skills

## Internship Reflection

This Data Analytics Internship helped me understand the complete end-to-end analytics lifecycle — from raw data preparation to business insights, dashboarding, statistical validation, and stakeholder communication.

Each task strengthened a different stage of the analytics process and helped me connect technical analysis with business decision-making.

---

## 1. Data Cleaning & Wrangling

I learned how to:

- Identify and handle missing values
- Detect duplicate records
- Validate data types
- Identify possible outliers
- Improve data consistency
- Create an analysis-ready dataset
- Prepare a structured data dictionary

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Excel

---

## 2. Exploratory Data Analysis

I strengthened my ability to:

- Perform descriptive statistics
- Analyze distributions
- Study revenue and customer patterns
- Analyze product and category performance
- Identify monthly sales trends
- Perform correlation analysis
- Convert findings into business insights

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn

---

## 3. SQL Business Analysis

I practiced writing SQL queries using:

- SELECT
- WHERE
- GROUP BY
- ORDER BY
- Aggregate functions
- Filtering
- Multi-table JOINs

This helped me understand how business questions can be translated into SQL queries.

**Tools:** SQL, SQLite

---

## 4. KPI Development

I learned how to define and calculate meaningful business KPIs including:

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Total Customers
- Revenue per Customer

This helped me understand that KPIs should always be connected to business objectives.

---

## 5. Customer Segmentation

I performed value-based customer segmentation into:

- High Value
- Medium Value
- Low Value

One of the strongest findings was that **High Value customers contributed 66.3% of total revenue**.

This showed how segmentation can support customer retention, targeting, and revenue growth strategies.

---

## 6. Interactive Dashboard Development

I developed an interactive Power BI dashboard containing:

- KPI cards
- Date filters
- Category filters
- Customer Segment filters
- City filters
- Reset Filters functionality
- Monthly revenue analysis
- Product performance
- Customer segmentation
- Geographic performance

**Tools:** Microsoft Power BI, Power Query, DAX

---

## 7. Statistical Validation

I learned how statistical testing can be used to validate business assumptions.

I performed a **Welch's Independent Samples T-Test** to test whether customer gender significantly affected average order sales.

### Results

- P-value: 0.4950
- Significance Level: 0.05
- Decision: Fail to Reject the Null Hypothesis

This helped me understand:

- Null and Alternative Hypotheses
- P-values
- Confidence Intervals
- Statistical Significance
- Business Interpretation of Statistical Results

**Tools:** Python, SciPy

---

## 8. Data Storytelling

I learned how to structure analytical findings into a clear business narrative:

**Business Problem → Analysis → Insight → Recommendation → Action**

This helped me improve my ability to communicate analytical findings to non-technical stakeholders.

---

## 9. Business Recommendations

The analysis helped me develop recommendations related to:

- Retaining High Value customers
- Growing Medium Value customers
- Prioritizing high-performing products
- Strengthening top geographic markets
- Using behavioral customer targeting
