The complete project was developed across four major analytical stages:

Focused on KPI development, customer segmentation, deeper business analysis, and development of an interactive Power BI dashboard.

### Task 4 — Data Storytelling & Statistical Validation
Focused on converting analytical findings into a business story and statistically validating key assumptions using hypothesis testing.

## Major Business Insight

Customer segmentation identified a highly concentrated revenue pattern:

| Customer Segment | Customers | Revenue Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| High Value | 316 | 66.3% |
| Medium Value | 315 | 26.3% |
| Low Value | 316 | 7.4% |

**Key Insight:** Nearly one-third of customers generate approximately two-thirds of total revenue.

---

## Statistical Validation

A **Welch's Independent Samples T-Test** was performed to determine whether customer gender significantly affects average order sales.

- P-value: **0.4950**
- Significance Level: **0.05**
- Decision: **Fail to Reject H₀**

### Conclusion

There was no statistically significant difference in average order sales between male and female customers.

This suggests that customer value, purchasing behavior, product preferences, and geography are more useful for business targeting than gender alone.
