# 📊 Sales Deep-Dive & Customer Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a deep-dive analysis of sales and customer data to uncover revenue trends, customer behavior, product performance, and geographic insights.

The project combines **Python-based data analysis** with an **interactive Microsoft Power BI dashboard**, enabling users to explore key business metrics and make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance
- Identify high-value customer groups
- Evaluate customer contribution to total revenue
- Analyze monthly revenue trends
- Identify top-performing products
- Identify high-performing cities
- Build an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard
- Generate actionable business recommendations

## 💡 Key Business Insights

1. Total revenue reached approximately **₹13.94 Cr**.

2. The dataset contains approximately **1,000 orders from 947 customers**.

3. **High-value customers generate 66.3% of total revenue**, despite representing roughly one-third of the customer base.

4. Medium-value customers contribute **26.3% of revenue**, representing an important opportunity for upselling and customer development.

5. Low-value customers contribute approximately **7.4% of total revenue**.

6. Product-level analysis identifies the products generating the highest revenue.

7. Geographic analysis highlights the strongest revenue-generating cities.

8. Monthly revenue analysis provides visibility into sales fluctuations and business performance over time.

## 💼 Business Recommendations

### 🎯 Retain High-Value Customers
Develop loyalty programs, personalized offers, and targeted engagement strategies.

### 📈 Convert Medium-Value Customers
Use cross-selling, upselling, and personalized recommendations to increase customer value.

### 🤝 Improve Low-Value Customer Engagement
Introduce targeted promotions and analyze purchasing behavior to encourage repeat purchases.

### 🏆 Prioritize Top Products
Maintain adequate inventory and increase promotional efforts for high-performing products.

### 🌍 Optimize Geographic Strategy
Focus sales and marketing resources on strong-performing cities while identifying opportunities in developing markets.

### 📊 Monitor Business KPIs
Continuously monitor revenue, orders, customer behavior, and sales trends using the interactive dashboard.
