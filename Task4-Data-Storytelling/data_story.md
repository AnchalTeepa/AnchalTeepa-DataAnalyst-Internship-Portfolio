# Sales Analytics Data Story

## 1. Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze sales and customer data to understand revenue performance, customer behavior, product trends, geographic performance, and key factors that can support better business decisions.

The analysis combines data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, customer segmentation, dashboarding, and statistical validation.

---

## 2. From Raw Data to Reliable Insights

The project started with data cleaning and preparation.

The dataset was checked for:

- Missing values
- Duplicate records
- Data consistency
- Incorrect or incomplete values
- Outliers
- Data types

After cleaning, a structured dataset was prepared for further analysis.

This ensured that the insights and recommendations were based on reliable and consistent data.

---

## 3. Overall Business Performance

The sales analysis identified the following key performance indicators:

- Total Revenue: ₹13.94 Cr
- Total Orders: 1,000
- Average Order Value: ₹139K
- Total Customers: 947
- Revenue per Customer: ₹147K

These KPIs provide an overall view of business performance and customer contribution.

---

## 4. Product Performance

Product-level analysis showed that a small group of products contributed strongly to overall revenue.

Top-performing products included:

1. Laptop
2. Mobile
3. Book
4. Rice
5. Chair

Laptop generated approximately ₹2.54 Cr in revenue and was the highest revenue-generating product.

This indicates that high-performing products should receive greater attention in inventory planning, promotions, and sales strategies.

---

## 5. Category Performance

Electronics emerged as the highest revenue-generating category.

Products such as laptops and mobile phones were among the strongest contributors to overall sales.

This suggests that Electronics is a strategically important category for the business.

---

## 6. Customer Segmentation

Customers were divided into three value-based segments:

| Customer Segment | Customers | Revenue Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| High Value | 316 | 66.3% |
| Medium Value | 315 | 26.3% |
| Low Value | 316 | 7.4% |

One of the most important findings was that High Value customers generated approximately two-thirds of total business revenue.

This demonstrates that customer value is not evenly distributed across the customer base.

## 7. Customer Strategy

Because High Value customers contribute 66.3% of revenue, customer retention should be a major business priority.

Possible strategies include:

- Loyalty programs
- Personalized offers
- Premium customer benefits
- Early access to promotions
- Targeted product recommendations

Medium Value customers also represent an opportunity for growth and can be targeted with strategies designed to move them toward the High Value segment.

- Inventory planning

## 10. Statistical Validation

To determine whether customer gender significantly influences average order sales, an independent samples Welch's t-test was performed.

### Hypotheses

H₀:
There is no statistically significant difference in average order sales between male and female customers.

H₁:
There is a statistically significant difference in average order sales between male and female customers.

### Results

- T-statistic: 0.6826
- P-value: 0.4950
- Significance Level: 0.05

Since the p-value is greater than 0.05, the null hypothesis was not rejected.

The analysis found no statistically significant difference in average order sales between male and female customers.

---

## 11. Statistical Business Interpretation

Although male customers showed slightly higher average sales, the difference was not statistically significant.

Therefore, gender alone should not be treated as a strong factor for sales targeting.

The business should focus more on customer value, purchasing behavior, product preferences, and geographic patterns.

## 12. Key Business Insights

The analysis produced five major insights:

1. The business generated approximately ₹13.94 Cr in total revenue.

2. High Value customers represent nearly one-third of customers but contribute approximately 66.3% of total revenue.

3. Laptop, Mobile, and Book are among the strongest revenue-generating products.

4. Electronics is a key revenue-driving category.

5. Statistical testing indicates that gender alone does not significantly influence average order value.

---

## 13. Business Recommendations

### Recommendation 1 — Retain High Value Customers

Develop loyalty and personalized engagement strategies for High Value customers because they contribute the majority of revenue.

### Recommendation 2 — Grow Medium Value Customers

Use targeted promotions and cross-selling strategies to increase spending among Medium Value customers.

### Recommendation 3 — Prioritize High-Performing Products

Maintain strong inventory availability and promotional support for top-performing products.

### Recommendation 4 — Focus on Strong Geographic Markets

Use location-specific marketing strategies in top-performing cities while identifying growth opportunities in weaker markets.

### Recommendation 5 — Use Behavioral Segmentation

Customer targeting should focus more on purchasing behavior and customer value rather than gender-based assumptions.

---

## 14. Call to Action

The business should adopt a customer-centric growth strategy focused on:

- Retaining High Value customers
- Converting Medium Value customers into High Value customers
- Promoting top-performing products
- Improving regional targeting
- Using data-driven customer segmentation

By focusing resources on the strongest revenue drivers, the business can improve customer retention, marketing efficiency, and long-term revenue growth.
